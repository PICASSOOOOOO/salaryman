ean Claw Automation Network — Feature Expansion Brief
Company: Picasso | Product: Salaryman

1. Expanded Claw Bot Agents (Open Claw Network)
We need a full suite of autonomous AI agents, each specialized and named, operating visually within the platform 24/7. These agents should be visible, animated, and active at all times — referred to internally as Replicants. They never stop working. They need to pay for themselves through the revenue they generate. All agents report up through Pablo → Jean Claw → Specialized Agents.

Proposed agents include:

Music Bot — Generates and publishes AI music continuously across platforms
E-Commerce Bot — Manages automated storefronts on Etsy, eBay, and other marketplaces
Communications Bot — Handles all inbound and outbound communications across email, chat, and phone
Finance Bot — Tracks company spending, incoming revenue, payroll, and side ventures in real time
Viber/Store Agent — Manages storefront operations and customer interactions
Business Development Bot — Identifies every viable automation business opportunity, builds it from scratch, and begins outreach immediately
All agents must work together as a unified system and be visualized on screen with live movement and activity indicators.

2. Technology Stack Recommendations
Claude Opus 1.6 — Primary language model for high-reasoning tasks
Gemini 1.5 (Google) — Supporting model for design and multimodal tasks
Nana Banana Pro — Game redesign and art direction
OpenRouter — Unified API routing across models
Codex Subscription — Code generation and automation support
Gusto Payroll API — Real payroll integration for employee salary tracking
Bank APIs — Real-time financial data syncing for revenue and spending metrics
