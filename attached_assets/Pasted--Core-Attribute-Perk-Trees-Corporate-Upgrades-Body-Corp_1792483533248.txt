
Core Attribute Perk Trees (Corporate Upgrades)

• Body (Corporate Fatigue & Physicality): Focuses on surviving brutal 14-hour shifts, consuming extreme amounts of caffeine, maximizing posture support from ergonomic chairs, and managing raw physical intimidation in the breakroom.
• Reflexes (Keyboard Agility & Speed): Focuses on typing speeds (WPM), rapid window-switching when the manager walks by, sprinting to the last clean coffee mug, and dodging impromptu meetings in the hallway.
• Technical Ability (IT & Spreadsheet Sorcery): Focuses on hacking the broken office printer, bypassing restricted website blocks, creating complex Excel macros, and maximizing the performance of your dual-monitor setup.
• Intelligence (Office Politics & Gossip): Focuses on navigating corporate jargon, BCC-ing the right executives, extracting office rumors, shifting blame during project failures, and weaponizing calendar invites.
• Cool (Stealth & Burnout Resistance): Focuses on taking long bathroom breaks without being noticed, leaving exactly at 5:00 PM undetected, crying in the stairwell quietly, and maintaining a perfect "corporate smile" during terrible presentations.

Skill Progression Trees (Passive Career Tracks)

These skills level up passively the more you perform these specific actions at your desk:
• Inbox Warrior: Leveled up via answering emails, clearing your inbox, and typing "Per my last email." Unlocks bonuses to passive-aggressive phrasing and faster auto-reply cool-downs.
• Printer Whisperer: Leveled up via clearing paper jams, replacing toner cartridges, and hitting the side of the machine. Unlocks higher success rates for tech repairs without calling IT.
• Stairwell Phantom: Leveled up via avoiding eye contact, hiding behind cubicle walls, and taking longer lunch breaks. Offers reduced visibility to upper management.
• Coffee Addict: Leveled up via brewing pots of coffee and raiding the snack cabinet. Grants temporary speed boosts and reduces the impact of the afternoon crash.
• Synergy Specialist: Leveled up via using words like "touch base," "circle back," and "low-hanging fruit" in meetings. Enhances team morale manipulation.
