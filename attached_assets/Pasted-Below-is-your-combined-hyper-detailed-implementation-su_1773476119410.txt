# Minx City / Salaryman – Full Implementation Design Document

## Project Vision

Minx City is a **persistent city simulation combined with a professional business terminal system**.

The project blends:

* urban life simulation
* economic progression
* survival mechanics
* AI terminal interaction
* RPG dialogue systems
* social multiplayer interaction

The design goal is to create a **dense, living city ecosystem** where players work, live, explore, and interact with both **the physical city and a digital professional terminal**.

The environment must feel **active, populated, and reactive at all times**.

---

# World Layout and Map Design

## Overall Map Structure

The world centers around **Minx City**, a dense coastal metropolis.

Design rules:

* The city must feel **compact and walkable**.
* Avoid large empty areas.
* Buildings must be **close together**.
* Major locations must be **relatively close in travel time**.

The city must contain **at least 12 sectors**.

Each sector should be approximately **10,000 square pixels or larger**.

---

## Geographic Hierarchy

The map structure must follow:

**Sector → District → Hamlet**

### Sectors

* Largest map regions
* Minimum of 12 sectors

### Districts

* Subsections within sectors

### Hamlets

* Neighborhood-level locations

Example address structure:

**Sector 3 → Industrial District → Dockside Hamlet**

---

# Roads and Street Layout

Roads must follow strict placement rules.

Developers must:

* Make roads **wide**
* Align roads **flush against buildings**
* Ensure buildings sit directly against the street edge
* Place **streetlights evenly along roads**

The city layout should resemble a **tight urban grid**.

Roads must allow **faster travel than sidewalks**.

---

# Transportation Systems

## Subway

The city includes a **subway network**.

Requirements:

* Multiple subway entrances across sectors
* Each entrance connects to sector destinations
* Entering a subway triggers a **travel sequence**

Travel cost:

**500 fiat per ride**

The subway should connect **major sectors and districts**.

---

## Vehicles

Players must be able to:

* purchase vehicles
* steal vehicles

Vehicles should:

* travel fastest on roads
* move slower off-road

Vehicles are intended for **longer travel distances**.

# Health System (HUD Simplification)

The current health format (example: **350 / 100**) must be removed.

This two-number system is confusing.

## New Health Display

The HUD should display **one single health number only**.

Example:

**350**

This number represents the **player's current health value**.

Maximum health should **not appear as a second number**.

---

## Health Color System

The health number must be **slightly larger than other HUD text**.

Health status should be communicated through **color changes**.

Color stages:

**Green**

Player health is strong.

**Yellow**

Player has taken moderate damage.

**Orange**

Player health is dangerously low.

**Red**

Player is critically injured.

**Dead**

Health reaches zero and the player dies.

---

## Health Regeneration Rules

Health regenerates when:

* the player is idle
* the player is not in combat

Food and rest can help restore health.
