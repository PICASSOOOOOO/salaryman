name: Shadow Tower hierarchy
description: Canonical vertical identity and occupancy rules for the Shadow Tower directory.
---

The Shadow Tower is a 67-floor vertical destination. Public services occupy the lower floors, new users and organizations must have room to establish themselves below the executive levels, Picasso Org is on floor 66, and Shadow Corp owns the restricted top floor 67. The top floor is not a person or alias.

**Why:** The tower needs visible room for community growth and a clear corporate hierarchy; a short tower made the world feel full before new users could arrive.

**How to apply:** Keep floor listings, elevator directories, access rules, pricing, and future playable geometry aligned to this hierarchy. Floors without an occupant should be explicitly labeled UNDER CONSTRUCTION or FOR SALE / LEASE, with a higher vertical premium on higher floors. Floors 40, 52, and 65 are reserved sealed/unlisted floors for future discovery content and are not commercially claimable.
