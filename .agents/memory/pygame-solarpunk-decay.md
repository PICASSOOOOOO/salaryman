---
name: Pygame solar-punk decay arc
description: Defines the visual progression of the downloadable SALARYMAN desktop experience.
---

The downloadable Pygame interface should be a bright, gamified solar-punk environment at the beginning. It must not start dark, noir, sterile, or already ruined. Visual decay is a progression tied to worsening conditions.

**Why:** The contrast between an optimistic living workplace and its later deterioration is part of the experience. Starting dark removes the emotional and gameplay impact of decay.

**How to apply:** Begin with daylight, plants, clean surfaces, lively color, readable game feedback, and optimistic motion. As decay advances, progressively introduce wear, failing systems, reduced plant health, harsher lighting, muted color, clutter, and environmental instability. Preserve usability throughout.
