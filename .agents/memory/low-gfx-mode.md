---
name: Low graphics mode
description: The single user-facing performance switch and its fidelity contract.
---

Low-GFX is an opt-in fallback for constrained devices; normal mode must preserve SALARYMAN's high-resolution art, detailed design, animation, and office simulation. Keep one canonical toggle in Settings and let it fan out through the existing low-gfx event/class.

**Why:** The visual experience is as important as functionality, so performance work must not silently flatten the art or reduce quality for capable devices. One setting avoids contradictory controls.

**How to apply:** Use the existing low-gfx signal for shader/effect reductions and bounded canvas work. Keep gameplay, office interactions, automation, and paid-feature behavior functional in low mode; never make low mode the default.
