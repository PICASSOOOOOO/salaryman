---
name: Cyber Deck licensing and evidence boundary
description: User-approved licensing and privacy constraints for the browser Cyber Deck.
---

Use the official Meshtastic JavaScript browser SDK under GPL-3.0-only. Provide the full license text and corresponding app source when distributing the browser application. Keep evidence files client-side and in-memory; radio connection and broadcasts must be explicitly initiated by the user.

**Why:** The user chose the official GPL-3.0 integration after being informed of its source-sharing obligations and requested browser-only forensic and mesh communication features.

**How to apply:** Preserve the GPL notice and source-availability requirement for future browser releases. Do not send evidence to SALARYMAN servers or transmit radio messages automatically.
