---
name: Contract delivery paper trail
description: SALARYMAN's contract drafting, client records, and external signing handoff.
---

SALARYMAN may prepare a client email and save the contract with the counterparty's contact details. Users can send through their chosen provider and record the provider, sent time, and reference/envelope ID. Do not imply Acrobat Sign, LegalZoom, or another provider API is connected unless an actual integration is configured.

**Why:** The user said email is an acceptable legal paper trail in many cases and a provider/email reference is sufficient; avoid blocking contract preparation on an e-sign integration.

**How to apply:** Keep sending user-initiated, preserve the contract and contact together, and distinguish a manual delivery record from provider-confirmed signature status.
