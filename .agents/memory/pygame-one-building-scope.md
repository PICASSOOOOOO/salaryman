---
name: Pygame one-building scope
description: Current playable Pygame work stays inside one building while its objects, NPCs, economy, and art are made coherent.
---

The current gameplay milestone is intentionally limited to one building. Do not expand Pygame into a city, multi-building world, or broad travel loop until the single building is complete and reliable.

The one building should still feel like a complete living system: physical stations, NPC routines and needs, work types, food/water, wealth, business operations, API persistence, and production-quality solar-punk art.

**Why:** The user wants depth and coherence in one building before breadth; expanding geography now would hide unfinished object and gameplay connections.

**How to apply:** Scope future API, object-manifest, artwork, and gameplay tasks to one authoritative building and its interior/exterior objects. Treat multi-building expansion as out of scope.
