---
name: Replicant power lore
description: Distinguishes human stamina from replicant-only powers and guides careful portrayal of prejudice.
---

Humans cannot use power; only replicants can. Replicants are rare in the building and face prejudice. Do not treat power as a human ability or make prejudice a joke.

NPCs and androids are people in the story, not lesser agents by default. Build
them with human-level agency and complexity. The story should seriously explore
android equality, what someone with a heart does under exploitation, and the
conflict between the rich and poor, including revolt.

The user's science-fiction reference points are Blade Runner and the works of
Arthur C. Clarke, Philip K. Dick, and Isaac Asimov. Treat these as inspiration
for the original story and worldbuilding.

**Why:** The user clarified this as a core setting rule and specifically asked for careful handling.

**How to apply:** Keep ordinary stamina and consumables separate from power.
Gate any power action to explicitly identified replicants; portray
discrimination critically and with care. Give NPCs and androids meaningful
agency, and handle class conflict and revolt as serious story themes.
