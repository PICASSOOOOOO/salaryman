---
name: Building-only mode
description: The current product boundary while SALARYMAN concentrates on one tower and its occupied floors.
---

SALARYMAN is currently building-only: the exterior city, transit, open-world movement, and exterior events are disabled so the office, tower floors, automation, business operations, and paid systems can become rich and reliable. This is a reversible product boundary, not a deletion of the future city concept.

**Legal rule:** city entry must require a valid player visa when the exterior reopens. Keep the visa schema, application routes, and server-side WebSocket visa gate intact; never treat the current lock as permission to bypass or erase visas.

**Data rule:** a building-only purge may remove transient exterior events, positions, transit fixtures/history, and liberation state, but must preserve player identity, businesses, organizations, money, billing, tower floors, office/property ownership, automation, and visa records.

**Why:** one occupied floor can receive the detailed simulation budget; simulating an empty city currently adds memory, storage, and failure surface without advancing the core automation-office product.

**How to apply:** route exterior links back to the tower/office, stop exterior WebSocket/event/transit workloads, and add future in-building lodging, infirmary, consumables, and automation features without reactivating city simulation.
