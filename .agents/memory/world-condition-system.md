---
name: World condition system
description: Physical solar-punk places degrade over months through localized material wear and recover through bounded repair work.
---

The world condition model has five ordered states: flourishing, maintained, weathered, neglected, and rotting. Condition is server-owned, drifts slowly from the last maintenance timestamp, and repair work raises the current score rather than resetting unrelated world state.

**Why:** The dark noir direction should be a consequence of consolidated power and neglect, not a futuristic cyberpunk material style. Players need to recognize care, abandonment, and recovery in ordinary real-world surfaces.

**How to apply:** Keep the same silhouette, footprint, collision, camera, animation timing, and interaction target across states. Change only localized material wear, plants, clutter, stains, lighting, and repair evidence; never use holograms, glowing circuitry, neon sludge, or global hue/grayscale filters. Extend the same contract to Tower floors, offices, and open neighborhoods.
