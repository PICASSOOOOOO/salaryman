---
name: One-building mini-city milestone
description: The first playable building must be a complete story-driven mini-city before SALARYMAN expands geographically.
---

The one-building milestone is not a small office demo. It should contain a believable mini-city loop: arrival, reception, lockdown, multiple meaningful spaces/stations, NPC routines and needs, work/business systems, services, progression, and a coherent story.

The building should feel like a game employees want to explore and return to. Godot owns real-time movement and action mechanics (including sprint, jump, and fighting/sweep moves); Python/business systems and the React browser experience remain connected through the existing APIs.

The first-user experience should remain cinematic and diegetic: a new user arrives under lockdown, is guided through the building by reception/animation, discovers the building one cue at a time, and unlocks access through story progress without dead-end screens.

Reuse the established onboarding, receptionist, elevator, office-discovery, story-gate, and post-onboarding cinematic contracts instead of creating a parallel intro flow. The lockdown should be communicated through the building's doors, lighting, announcements, and access state—not by hiding the playable surface.

**Why:** Previous work expanded breadth before one place had enough depth, leaving disconnected systems and placeholder presentation. The owner wants this building to be a polished vertical slice that proves the complete game loop and is enjoyable for workers to explore.

**How to apply:** Reject city expansion until this single building has an end-to-end new-user intro, Godot-driven playable movement/actions, persistent objects/economy, NPC life, authoritative API connections, story progression, and production-quality solar-punk art.

Keep work on this first building until its complete loop is polished before
starting another building or geographic expansion. Building ownership should
support organization-hired NPC or human workers whose physical construction,
repair, electrical, concrete, plumbing, decay remediation, renovation, and
customization work takes time and is animated live. Hiring, firing, permissions,
and the work itself should generate coherent stories and reusable in-game
income loops.

**Why:** the user confirmed that one-building-first is the product strategy and
that physical building work, organization hiring, time, animation, and stories
are core economic activities.
