---
name: Site-wide popup boundary
description: The product-wide rule for notification pop-ups and COMMS access.
---

Automatic COMMS message and notification pop-ups are opt-in, not default-on. Keep unread counts visible, and open COMMS in its dedicated page rather than a floating overlay. Preserve dialogs that users deliberately open for forms, confirmations, or sign-in.

**Why:** The user said, “Avoid pop ups. Site wide.” The default-on alerts and floating COMMS panel can interrupt text entry.

**How to apply:** Use the shared COMMS alert preference for automatic incoming-message/notification toasts across routes. Keep badges and in-page status; do not add floating chat overlays. Keep necessary user-initiated dialogs.
