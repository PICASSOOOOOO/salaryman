- Building danger should come from nearby hostile actors. The first intended temporary encounter is an open basement room with rats and snakes; ordinary building areas are usually safe.
- In-building combat is limited to nonlethal fistfights. Do not introduce weapons or full-murder combat.
- Pest companies can create cleanup work orders for players and NPCs to go downstairs and resolve.
- When hostile actors are nearby, choose a suitable track randomly from the existing soundtrack pool, play it quietly, and fade it with distance. This cue overrides/stops the proximity track from a radio that the player purchased and physically placed; do not auto-place or globally play a radio.

**Why:** the user stated these product rules while defining the downloadable game's danger and audio behavior.

**How to apply:** Use this when implementing in-building threats, pest cleanup work, or local radio/music behavior. Keep the response nonlethal and proximity-driven.
