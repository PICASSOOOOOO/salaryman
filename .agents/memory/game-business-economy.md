name: Game business licensing and economy
description: User-defined rules for in-game business registration, licensing, credit, hiring, and operating taxes.
---

In-game business registration is separate from Business Hub organizations. Real businesses need browser-side legitimacy proof. Legal fictional businesses require work history, expertise, local economic capacity, and business-specific minimum net worth; net worth means the player's personal total worth, not just FIAT in their wallet. Illegal syndicates are licensed through an in-game illegal NPC who controls access to contracts and projects; eligibility depends on expertise and the character's credit/criminal profile. An organization's registered license limits which activities can earn income.

Credit is a game credit score affected by debt-to-income, income, work activity, and inactivity. Low credit should raise prices and close some opportunities while improving criminal abilities; high credit should favor conventional opportunities. Job qualification checks credit, experience, and work history. Job slots are finite and become available again after logout or timeout. Each business type should have its own operating tasks and strategies rather than a generic shop loop.

The user's tax policy is daily on gross earned income, not net profit or income after expenses: real businesses pay 5% on FIAT/GOLD, never service Credits; fictional businesses pay 10–20%, with higher net worth lowering the rate; only illegal business organizations pay 40% of their earned gross income. Theft proceeds are not taxed, even when stolen by or received through an illegal organization; ƒ100,000 of earned income in an illegal organization means ƒ40,000 tax, but ƒ100,000 stolen means no tax. Unpaid taxes enter collections; after three days the organization closes and its owner cannot qualify for another business for three days.

**Why:** the user clarified that business creation, criminal access, credit, staffing, and taxation should form a distinct in-game economy rather than reuse the real-world Business Hub as-is, and clarified that tax base means daily gross earned income.

**How to apply:** keep real-world organization records separate from game business records. Use server-authoritative eligibility and income sources, and preserve the distinct legal, fictional, and illegal rules when building registration, hiring, storefront, credit, or tax flows.
