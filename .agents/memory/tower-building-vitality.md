Treat the Tower as a character-like entity, not only as a backdrop: it needs electrical power, plumbing, and ongoing upkeep to survive. Building electrical power is separate from replicant power and player stamina.

The default downloadable game is an MMO: all normal sessions connect to the same server, and building condition is shared across players. Custom servers built through GitHub are not hosted unless requested.

Pablo Corp is responsible for Tower power and water bills. Building service sales
and company-owned property sales or leases fund those costs; unpurchased office
inventory belongs to Pablo Corp, while resale and sublease proceeds after a
player purchase belong to that owner. Low operating funds raise Tower prices,
and unpaid bills can disable power and water. Emergency backup serves paid
clients and wealthy players first, then shared building infrastructure. Apply
these economic rules to the current single-building maze, not the old city-wide
simulation. Property settlement remains server-authoritative and supports
ATM-transferred income, FIAT, and GOLD.

**Why:** The user described the building as a living part of the game that players keep alive together, and clarified the shared-server model.

**How to apply:** Keep shared utility/upkeep state server-owned and visible across normal sessions; keep custom-server hosting opt-in. Do not conflate building electricity with character abilities.

**Why:** The user clarified that Pablo Corp owns unpurchased Tower inventory and pays the building's utility costs, and that backup eligibility prioritizes paid clients and wealthy players before infrastructure.

**How to apply:** Credit company-owned sales and leases to Tower operations, preserve private resale proceeds, and keep the cashflow, price pressure, outages, and backup policy local to this one building.
