Keep the browser/control-plane UI modern and separate from the downloadable game's art direction. In the Godot game, camera-transition feature panels may use a clean green-on-black, typewriter-like interface with keyboard commands, contextual device labels, category tabs, and crisp frame lines. Keep the panel model consistent across floors; floor-specific variation belongs in the interiors. Do not extend this terminal UI treatment to the general HUD or office builder/customization.

ASCII motifs are part of the game's visual identity and may appear beyond feature panels in selected world-art details and arcade displays. Use them purposefully for distinctive signage, display imagery, or small decorative marks; do not turn the entire world into dense text or reduce gameplay readability.

CRT-inspired display effects are allowed only on physical terminal and television screens. Keep effects attached to their screen surfaces; do not apply scanlines, phosphor glow, flicker, grain, or chromatic aberration to feature panels, arcade screens, the general HUD, or broad world surfaces.

**Why:** The user asked for ASCII overlays to add a unique style to the game, including more prominent use in other areas, and clarified that CRT effects are acceptable when restricted to terminal screens and TVs.

**How to apply:** Use ASCII as a selective design motif in the Godot world and feature panels. Limit CRT effects to terminal/TV displays and keep other screens and interface surfaces crisp.
