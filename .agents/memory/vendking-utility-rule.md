---
name: VendKing utility rule
description: Defines the gameplay-value and economic requirements for Shadow Tower vending stock.
---

VendKing inventory must provide a real, server-authoritative player benefit: stamina recovery, limited-use work-income boosts, skill preparation, practical protection, or low-cost automation. High-value training can cost extreme amounts of FIAT, but an item may not be an expensive cosmetic with no effect.

**Why:** Players spend earned FIAT on vending stock, so every listing must materially change what their character can do or earn. Combat weapons remain exclusive to physical Black Market interactions.

**How to apply:** Give every new vending item a visible effect and enforce it on the server. If low-end bots are added, disclose their ongoing real-world operating cost and keep their capabilities narrow enough to remain inexpensive to run.
