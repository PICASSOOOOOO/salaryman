---
name: VendKing utility rule
description: Defines the gameplay-value and economic requirements for Shadow Tower vending stock.
---

VendKing inventory must provide a real, server-authoritative player benefit: stamina recovery, limited-use work-income boosts, skill preparation, practical protection, or low-cost automation. High-value training can cost extreme amounts of FIAT, but an item may not be an expensive cosmetic with no effect. Purchases must respect character stamina, wallet, and net worth; these are survival lifelines, not cosmetic stats.

Interior threats are gameplay, not set dressing: bankruptcy, debt, and collectors must be consequential; bankers and litigation actors may use deceptive ploys; security can respond when crimes such as pickpocketing, theft, or fraud are witnessed. The undercover dealer is the fictional procurer of rare/illicit goods and services, information, news, keys, diversions, and theft. No player or dealer weapons are allowed inside the Tower; only security carries batons. Debt collectors are unarmed and rely on cunning tactics.

**Why:** The user requires both vending purchases and economic/security threats to have real in-game consequences, and specified the Tower's unarmed rule. Players spend earned FIAT on vending stock, so every listing must materially change what their character can do or earn.

**How to apply:** Give every new vending item a visible effect and enforce it on the server. Preserve the wallet/net-worth/debt rules when purchases settle. Put the black-market dealer in the B4–B6 tunnel maze and verify proximity to that NPC server-side before trade. Keep all Tower visitors and illicit contractors unarmed; security batons are role-restricted. Make debt collectors consequential through cunning, non-weapon tactics. Implement banker, litigation, collector, and security actors as stateful gameplay threats with actionable outcomes, not ambient NPCs. If low-end bots are added, disclose their ongoing real-world operating cost and keep their capabilities narrow enough to remain inexpensive to run.
