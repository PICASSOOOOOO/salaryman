---
name: VendKing utility rule
description: Defines the gameplay-value and economic requirements for Shadow Tower vending stock.
---

VendKing inventory must provide a real, server-authoritative player benefit: stamina recovery, limited-use work-income boosts, skill preparation, practical protection, or low-cost automation. High-value training can cost extreme amounts of FIAT, but an item may not be an expensive cosmetic with no effect. Purchases must respect character stamina, wallet, and net worth; these are survival lifelines, not cosmetic stats.

Interior threats are gameplay, not set dressing: bankruptcy, debt, and collectors must be consequential; bankers and litigation actors may use deceptive ploys; security can respond when crimes such as pickpocketing, theft, or fraud are witnessed.

**Why:** The user requires both vending purchases and economic/security threats to have real in-game consequences. Players spend earned FIAT on vending stock, so every listing must materially change what their character can do or earn. Combat weapons remain exclusive to physical Black Market interactions.

**How to apply:** Give every new vending item a visible effect and enforce it on the server. Preserve the wallet/net-worth/debt rules when purchases settle. Implement banker, litigation, collector, and security actors as stateful gameplay threats with actionable outcomes, not ambient NPCs. If low-end bots are added, disclose their ongoing real-world operating cost and keep their capabilities narrow enough to remain inexpensive to run.
