---
name: VendKing utility rule
description: Defines the gameplay-value and economic requirements for Shadow Tower vending stock.
---

VendKing inventory must provide a real, server-authoritative player benefit: stamina recovery, limited-use work-income boosts, skill preparation, practical protection, or low-cost automation. High-value training can cost extreme amounts of FIAT, but an item may not be an expensive cosmetic with no effect. Purchases must respect character stamina, wallet, and net worth; these are survival lifelines, not cosmetic stats.

Interior threats are gameplay, not set dressing: bankruptcy, debt, and collectors must be consequential; bankers and litigation actors may use deceptive ploys; security can respond when crimes such as pickpocketing, theft, or fraud are witnessed. The undercover dealer is a mysterious former personal security consultant to Pablo who now blends into the Tower like a ghost. He procures rare/illicit goods, drugs, information, news, and keys directly; hitmen, influence, espionage, diversions, and theft use a mix of purchases and missions. No player or dealer weapons are allowed inside the Tower; only security carries batons. Debt collectors are unarmed and rely on cunning tactics.

**Why:** The user requires both vending purchases and economic/security threats to have real in-game consequences, specified the Tower's unarmed rule, and established the dealer's history and mixed service model. Players spend earned FIAT on vending stock, so every listing must materially change what their character can do or earn.

**How to apply:** Give every new vending item a visible effect and enforce it on the server. Preserve the wallet/net-worth/debt rules when purchases settle. Put the dealer in the B4–B6 tunnel maze, render him at rotating basement/stairwell/rear-loading hideouts, and verify proximity server-side before trade or service. Keep all Tower visitors unarmed; security batons are role-restricted. Make debt collectors consequential through cunning, non-weapon tactics. Implement broker services as real purchases or playable missions with server-owned outcomes, not ambient copy. Implement banker, litigation, collector, and security actors as stateful gameplay threats with actionable outcomes. If low-end bots are added, disclose their ongoing real-world operating cost and keep their capabilities narrow enough to remain inexpensive to run.
