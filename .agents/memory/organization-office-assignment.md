The Tower has 67 floors; floors 5–54 are the 50 sale floors, with four office units each (200 before mergers). Adjacent units may be purchased together as one larger suite. The first 50 qualifying paid organization admins/owners may receive one complimentary organization office while their paid entitlement remains active; this is a narrow promotion, not a replacement for ordinary office purchases. Offices are for owners/admins, not employees. Each includes one desk, terminal, chair, vending machine, and ATM. Employee workstation bundles are a separate organization expense and are assigned during onboarding. People without an office arrive in the shared lobby. The lobby connects to a shared hallway through a compact elevator cabin that carries observed live passengers and animates travel to their selected floor.

Browser-facing office and equipment UI uses business terms, not game terminology.

**Why:** The owner specified a 50-floor, 200-unit sale inventory, a limited paid-user office promotion, admin-only offices, paid employee equipment, and the lobby → elevator → hallway path.

**How to apply:** Use existing Tower price/access authority and organization FIAT accounts; never grant employees private office access by default. Keep the promotion capped and subscription-gated, make workstation charges atomic/idempotent, and distinguish observed occupants from stale/last-seen presence.
