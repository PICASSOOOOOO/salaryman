---
name: Organization office assignment and elevator
description: User-defined desktop building topology and when organizations receive a private office.
---

An organization gets a private office only after an office has been purchased and assigned; organization membership alone never creates a free room. People without an assigned organization or personal office arrive in the shared lobby. Purchased organization offices are unique per organization and shared with its eligible occupants. The lobby connects to a shared hallway through a compact elevator cabin that carries live passengers and animates travel to their selected floor; the hallway connects to other active offices. Preserve the existing economy and reward systems; office work must reuse their current purchase and reward paths.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose purchase-based room assignment, confirmed the economy and rewards are already in place, and described the lobby → elevator → hallway → office path.

**How to apply:** Resolve office access from the existing purchased Tower office/floor records and do not replace or bypass existing economy/reward behavior. Keep lobby and hallway shared, scope room presence to the assigned office, and distinguish observed passengers from stale/last-seen presence.
