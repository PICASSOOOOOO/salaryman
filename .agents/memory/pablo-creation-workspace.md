name: Pablo creation workspace

---

Pablo should have a dedicated creation workspace where users can organize and build apps, websites, games, and income ventures. The work area should connect SALARYMAN's internal tools with external references and services, and make project work visible and interactive instead of relying on long chat threads.

**Why:** the user explicitly wants a Replit-like creation area inside Pablo, combining internal work with external efforts; they also prefer minimal copy and interactive visual surfaces site-wide.

**How to apply:** keep projects and drafts persistent, show generated work in an editable/previewable area, and require deliberate review before external publishing, deployment, database changes, or financial settlement. Current income mode is review-only until a real revenue source is verified; any future real payout is opt-in for the business owner, never an employee capability.

## SALARYMAN product direction

The user's product goal is to make automation and communication a visual, gamified narrative centered on an office simulation for remote work and digital collaboration, powered by Pablo and SALARYMAN's automation features. The downloadable desktop app is the office-simulation hook and was unfinished as of 2026-10-05. The browser experience is intended to funnel users toward the download so they can experience that simulation.

SALARYMAN should also offer a free marketplace where users create features specifically for SALARYMAN, share them with other users, and install them into My Apps. My Apps should distinguish user-owned/private items from public/shared items. The creator audience includes people making video games and other interactive experiences, not only players of the downloadable game.

**Why:** the user explicitly described this as SALARYMAN's goal and manifesto and asked that it be remembered.

**How to apply:** prioritize the browser-to-desktop funnel, a complete office-simulation experience, and a free SALARYMAN-specific creator marketplace with Owned and Public collections. Treat the desktop app as unfinished unless current project status shows otherwise.
