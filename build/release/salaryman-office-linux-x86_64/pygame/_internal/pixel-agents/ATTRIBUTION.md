are sourced from **Pixel Agents** by Pablo De Lucca, used under the MIT

- Upstream: https://github.com/pablodelucca/pixel-agents

The Salaryman / Picasso.ai project uses these assets as a decorative pixel
office diorama in the Pablo onboarding modal and the Immigration intake page.
No modifications have been made to the sprite files themselves.
